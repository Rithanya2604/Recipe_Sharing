Our story

Cooking is better shared

YummyShare started as a simple question: why do so many good recipes stay trapped in one kitchen? We built a home for recipes from Indian, Italian, Japanese and Korean cooking traditions, written by people who actually cook them — and a tool to help you use up what's already in your fridge before it goes to waste.

Every recipe on this site includes real ingredient lists, numbered steps and a cooking video, because reading a recipe and being able to cook it are two different skills.
